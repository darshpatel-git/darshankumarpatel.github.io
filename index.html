PROFILE

About

Dr. Darshankumar Patel is an Assistant Professor in Civil Engineering at Shri Vile Parle Kelavani Mandal's Institute of Technology, Dhule.

His academic interests and scholarly work span urban planning, transportation planning, public transport accessibility, GIS mapping, sustainable highways, water resource management, construction materials and technology-enabled education.

He completed his Ph.D. in 2026, holds an M.Tech. in Urban Planning from VNIT Nagpur and a B.E. in Civil Engineering.
