01 / ABOUT

Engineering knowledge.
Real-world impact.

I am a Civil Engineering academician and researcher interested in applying analytical, geospatial and computational methods to practical engineering and urban challenges.

My academic journey brings together Civil Engineering, Urban Planning, Business Analytics and Deep Learning. My research explores how data-driven approaches can support more accessible public transportation, understand traffic congestion and strengthen environmental decision-making.

Alongside research, I am committed to teaching, mentoring students and developing interdisciplinary learning experiences that connect engineering fundamentals with emerging technologies.
